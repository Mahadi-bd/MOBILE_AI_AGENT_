LIFE EASY is a personal assistant app (tasks, notes, income/expense list, weather and an AI chat). This policy explains what happens to your information.

## 1. Information stored only on your phone
Your tasks, notes, income/expense entries and your app settings (assistant name, tone, language, your name, weather city, voice style) are saved **only on your device**. We have no account system and cannot see this data. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

**Your Gemini API key:** the app does not contain any AI key. You enter your own key in *Persona → AI settings*. It is stored encrypted on your phone (Android Keystore) and is only sent to Google to use the AI. We never receive it. You can delete it at any time in the same screen.

## 2. Information sent to other services when you use a feature
| Feature | What is sent | To whom | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI chat | The messages of the current conversation you typed or spoke, plus your chosen assistant role, tone and language, together with **your own API key** | Google (Gemini API) | To generate the answer |
| Voice input (microphone button) | Your voice is processed by the speech-recognition service of your Android phone (usually Google). The app itself does **not** record or store audio. | Google / your phone's speech service | To turn speech into text |
| Voice replies | The reply text is read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine | Your phone | To speak the answer |
| Weather | The city name you entered (default: Khulna) | Open-Meteo.com | To show the weather. No GPS / precise location is used. |
| Update check (only in the GitHub APK version) | A request to the GitHub API for the newest release number | GitHub | To tell you if a new version exists |

Please do not type passwords, bank details or other very sensitive information into the AI chat. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and the Gemini API terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
Open-Meteo's information: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms

## 3. Permissions
- **Internet** – needed for AI chat, voice recognition and weather.
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)** – only used when you tap the microphone button. You can deny it and keep typing instead.

## 4. What we do NOT do
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No selling or sharing of your personal data.
- No collection of your contacts, photos, files or precise location.

## 5. Children
LIFE EASY is not directed to children under 13. The AI chat may produce incorrect answers, so adult guidance is recommended for younger users. Using the Gemini API requires that the key owner meets Google's age requirements.

## 6. Deleting your data
Delete tasks/notes inside the app, or clear the app data / uninstall the app in Android settings to remove everything stored on your phone, including the saved API key. Text sent to the AI service is not linked to an account in this app.
